Understanding TIFF and JPG Formats

What is a TIFF File?

TIFF (Tagged Image File Format) is used for storing high-resolution images with no compression loss—ideal for printing and archiving.

Why Use JPG?

JPG (or JPEG) is a compressed image format that balances quality with file size, making it perfect for online use and sharing.
