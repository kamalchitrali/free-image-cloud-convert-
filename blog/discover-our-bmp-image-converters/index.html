1. BMP to PNG – Great for Web and Design

This one is good when you want to use images on websites or for designs. PNG is a special format because it can have transparent background. 4. BMP to ICO – Make Icons for Websites or Apps

You just take a picture in BMP, convert it to ICO, and use it. It makes your work look cool and special. Web developers and app makers will really like this.

5. BMP to WEBP – Best for Websites with Small Size

WebP is a new image format made by Google. It is very small in size but still good in quality. That means your website becomes faster because the images are light. I used it for my friend’s website, and it loaded much faster. If you are building a blog or online shop, you should use this BMP to WEBP converter. It will help your site work better.

6. BMP to TIFF – For Printing or Saving with Full Quality

TIFF is for people who care a lot about image quality. If you are printing a photo or keeping it safe for future, TIFF is a good format. It does not lose quality. The size is big, but the image stays perfect. I used it when I printed my art on a t-shirt. The print was very clear and sharp. So if you are an artist or working in design, you can try this tool.
