This tool is very smart. WEBP is a new format made by Google. It makes small image files but the quality is still good. I used it for my brother’s website and it loaded much faster. If you care about website speed, you should try PNG to WEBP tool.

This is for people who want the best image quality. TIFF is great for printing and saving important pictures. It does not lose quality. I used it to print my drawing for an art class. The print came out very clear. This tool is good for artists and professionals.
